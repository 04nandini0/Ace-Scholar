How the route is built

Step 1

Understand

Your question becomes an intent (attendance, marks, notes…) and a course name, only from your own words.

Step 2

Match the platform

Moodle, DigiCampus and Canvas each keep their own verified routes. A screenshot can confirm which one you are on.

Step 3

Map the path

You get a visual breadcrumb and numbered steps, trimmed to what is left from where you are now.

Step 4

Honest answers

If something is not mapped, or the screenshot is unclear, we say so instead of guessing.

About ACE-Scholar

Academic software shouldn't feel like a maze.

ACE-Scholar helps students navigate unfamiliar academic platforms without hunting through menus. Routes come from a verified navigation database, and AI is used to understand your question and to read screenshots, never to invent menu items or student data. Menus vary between institutions, so always trust what you see on your own screen.
